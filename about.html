About Riftline Reports

Riftline Reports is a concept blog for sci-fi enthusiasts exploring speculative theories about timelines, time machines, alien temporal signatures, and multiverse anomalies.

This website is built as a multi-page CSS assignment for CS106, demonstrating core styling concepts including selectors, modern layouts, pseudo-classes, responsive behavior, and form design.

Mission

Translate complex time-related sci-fi concepts into readable, engaging story posts.

Topics

Quantum defragmentation, time conspiracies, time-slip reports, Mandela echoes, and hidden chrono-tech.

Style Direction

Neon Crimson cyberpunk visuals with dark premium tones, glowing accents, and high-contrast typography.
